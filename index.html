    FAQ

    Got questions?
    We've got answers.

    Everything you need to know about hailO — from how it works to how to get started.

    How do I book a ride on hailo?+
    Open the app, enter your pickup and drop location, and choose bike, pink bike, auto, or car. You'll see the fare upfront before you confirm.
    What can I send using hailo Parcels?+
    Anything from a small envelope to bulk business loads. Choose a bike for quick, light sends or a tempo for bigger or business shipments.
    Is it safe to order medicines and prescriptions?+
    Yes. Every pharmacy order goes through verified partners. Upload your prescription in the app and our partners handle it with care before dispatch.
    How is my fare calculated?+
    Your fare is shown upfront based on distance and vehicle type, before you confirm your ride or delivery — so you always know the cost first.
    How do I track my ride or delivery?+
    Once confirmed, you'll see a tracking screen showing your rider or driver's location and estimated arrival, with updates at pickup, en route, and drop-off.
    What payment methods are accepted?+
    hailO supports cash on delivery and UPI payment options directly to the driver partner.
    Can I cancel a ride or order after booking?+
    Yes, you can cancel before a rider or driver is assigned, or shortly after — a small cancellation fee may apply. Once they're en route, you cannot cancel.
    How do I contact support if I have an issue?+
    Yes, you can cancel before a driver is assigned or shortly after. Once a driver is en route, cancellations may incur a small fee. You'll be prompted to select a reason to help us improve.
    How do I contact support if I have an issue?+
    Use the in-app "Contact Support" feature, email help@hailo.co.in, or call +91 788 788 8999. We're here 24/7.
