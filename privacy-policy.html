1. Introduction and Acceptance

S V Sanghvi Transtrade Techno Private Limited ("hailO", "the Company", "we", "our", or "us") operates the hailO platform, connecting Customers with goods- delivery, logistics and passenger ride-hailing services — including the women-only Pink Rides service — through a network of Driver Partners, District Transport Associates ("DTA") and Travel Associates ("TA"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we gather, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you. It applies to our website, our Customer and Driver Partner mobile applications (together, the "hailO Apps"), and to any offline interactions we may have with you, such as phone or email support, all of which are together referred to as the "Services".

This Privacy Policy applies to Customers, Driver Partners, District Transport Associates (DTAs), Travel Associates (TAs), visitors to our website, and any other person using or interacting with the Services.

This Policy forms part of, and should be read together with, hailO's Customer Terms and Conditions and the applicable Driver Partner, DTA and TA work agreements. By creating an account, booking a ride or delivery, or otherwise using the Services, you confirm that you have read and accepted this Policy. If any part of this Policy is unacceptable to you, please refrain from using the hailO Apps.

hailO may revise this Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law. Material changes will be indicated by updating the effective date at the top of this page, and, where required, we will notify you through the hailO Apps or by email.

2. Definitions

  • "Device" means any computer, smartphone, tablet or other equipment used to access the hailO Apps.
  • "Device Identifier" means the IP address, advertising ID or other unique identifier associated with a Device.
  • "Driver Partner" means an individual who provides delivery or ride-hailing Services through the hailO Apps, whether directly or through a DTA or TA.
  • "DTA" means a District Transport Associate engaged under a DTA Work Agreement to manage Driver Partners within a designated territory.
  • "TA" means a Travel Associate engaged under a TA Work Agreement to manage Driver Partners providing passenger ride-hailing Services.
  • "Personal Information" means any information that identifies or could reasonably be used to identify you, including your name, address, email and mobile number.
  • "Service Provider" means a third party engaged by hailO to perform functions on our behalf, such as payment processing, background verification, cloud hosting or customer support.
  • "Usage Data" means technical and behavioural data collected automatically as you interact with the hailO Apps, such as app-navigation patterns, session length and device information.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Give Us

When you register on the hailO Apps or otherwise interact with us, we may ask for information such as your full name, email address, mobile number, residential or delivery address, PIN code, and a password or authentication credential. Driver Partners, DTAs and TAs are additionally asked to furnish KYC documents, vehicle registration and insurance papers, pollution-certification, bank account details, and any other document reasonably needed to verify eligibility and roadworthiness.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

As you use the hailO Apps, our systems and those of our Service Providers automatically record certain Usage Data, including the browser or app version you use, pages or screens viewed, the source that directed you to the Apps, timestamps, crash logs and Device Identifiers. We rely on this data to keep the Apps stable, secure and responsive to how people actually use them.

3.3 Location Information

Where you permit location access, we collect precise or approximate GPS coordinates from your Device and, during an active trip or delivery, from the Driver Partner's Device. Most GPS-enabled phones can pinpoint a location to within roughly 15 metres. Location data is used to match you with nearby Driver Partners, calculate fares and the per-trip recharge amount, generate accurate pickup and drop instructions, support in-trip safety features, and produce trip receipts.

The hailO Driver App may continue to capture location data in the background during an active trip, even when the app is not in the foreground of the Driver Partner's Device. This location trail is archived together with the corresponding trip data and may be retained for an extended period. We use this archived location data to reconstruct and display the route taken by the Driver Partner between the pickup point and the drop point.

3.4 Mobile Application Data

Where the Services are accessed through a hailO App, we may additionally collect your device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, preferred language, and network carrier. We access only the OTP messages required to verify your mobile number or authenticate payments. We do not access, read or collect your personal SMS messages.

3.5 Application Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the hailO Apps may request access to your Device's location, camera, photo gallery, contacts, notifications and SMS (for OTP verification only). These permissions are used solely to provide and improve the Services. You may manage or revoke these permissions at any time through your Device settings, although doing so may limit certain functionality of the Services.

3.6 Calls, Chats and Support Interactions

Calls made to our support lines and in-app chat conversations may be recorded or logged for quality assurance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and Driver Partner training purposes.

3.7 Emergency Contacts, Trip-Sharing and Referrals

If you add an emergency contact within the hailO Apps, we store that person's name and phone number so that we can share your live trip status with them, including during a safety escalation. If you use a referral feature or book a trip on behalf of someone in your contacts, we collect and use the relevant contact's name and mobile number solely to complete that request or extend a referral invitation.

3.8 Information from Third Parties

We may supplement the information described above with data obtained from identity-verification and background-check agencies engaged on our behalf, payment partners, and, where legally permitted, publicly available sources, in order to validate the identity and eligibility of Customers and Driver Partners.

3.9 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and Apps use cookies and comparable technologies (such as local storage and SDK-based identifiers) to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, measure traffic patterns, and understand which features are most used. You can control cookies through your browser or Device settings; disabling them may limit certain functionality. We do not control cookies placed independently by third parties whose content or advertisements may appear within the Services, and this Policy does not extend to those third-party cookies.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • create and manage your account and authenticate you when you sign in;
  • connect Customers with available Driver Partners, DTAs and TAs and facilitate the requested delivery or ride, including Pink Rides;
  • calculate fares, process payments, and compute the per-trip recharge amount and associated DTA/TA commissions;
  • provide customer support, respond to queries, and resolve complaints or disputes;
  • send trip confirmations, receipts, safety alerts and other service-related communications;
  • monitor, troubleshoot and improve the performance, security and usability of the hailO Apps;
  • detect, investigate and prevent fraud, unauthorised access and other prohibited activity;
  • conduct internal analytics and research to improve service coverage and quality;
  • comply with applicable law, regulatory requests, and our contractual obligations; and
  • with your consent, send you promotional messages, offers or market-research communications by email, SMS, push notification or call.

You may opt out of promotional communications at any time, in line with the Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations, 2018, by using the in-app opt-out setting or by emailing help@hailo.co.in. Service-related communications necessary to deliver the Services will continue even if you opt out of marketing messages.

5. How We Share Your Information

5.1 With Driver Partners, DTAs and TAs

To fulfil a booking, we share the Customer's name, pickup/drop location and contact number with the assigned Driver Partner, and, where relevant, with the DTA or TA responsible for that Driver Partner, strictly for the purpose of completing the trip or delivery.

5.2 With Service Providers

We engage Service Providers to host our infrastructure, process payments, perform identity and background verification, provide customer support tooling, deliver SMS/email notifications, and carry out analytics. These Service Providers may access Personal Information only to the extent needed to perform their function for us, under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations no less protective than this Policy.

We may engage third-party technology providers for cloud hosting, analytics, mapping, crash reporting and communication services. These providers process information only on our behalf and in accordance with applicable law and contractual confidentiality obligations.

5.3 For Legal and Safety Reasons

We may disclose information to courts, regulators, law-enforcement authorities or other government agencies where required by applicable law, in response to a valid legal process, or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of hailO, our users, Driver Partners, DTAs, TAs or the public, or to investigate suspected fraud or illegal activity.

5.4 Business Transfers

If hailO is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, Personal Information may be transferred to the successor entity as part of that transaction, subject to that entity honouring the commitments made in this Policy.

5.5 With Your Consent

Where you choose to participate in a co-branded offer, contest, or promotion involving a named third party, or where you elect to share ride details with a chosen contact, the relevant information will be shared with that third party in accordance with the terms disclosed to you at the time.

5.6 Payment Processing

Payment transactions are processed between the Driver Partner and the Customer directly. hailO does not store any debit card, credit card or UPI credentials on its own systems.

We do not sell, rent or trade your Personal Information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain Personal Information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, to comply with our legal, tax and accounting obligations, and to resolve disputes. Where retention is no longer necessary, we securely delete or anonymise the information, subject to any longer retention period mandated by applicable law.

7. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your hailO account by contacting us through the hailO Apps or by emailing help@hailo.co.in. Upon receiving a valid request, we will delete or anonymise your Personal Information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by applicable law or is necessary for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, taxation, legal claims or regulatory compliance.

8. Data Security

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect Personal Information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction, in line with applicable data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and any rules, regulations or amendments made thereunder, as applicable. While we work to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and you play a role too — please keep your account credentials confidential and notify us promptly of any suspected unauthorised use of your account.

9. International Data Transfers

Your Personal Information may be processed or stored on servers located within or outside India where permitted by applicable law. Where such transfers occur, hailO will implement reasonable safeguards to ensure that your Personal Information continues to receive an appropriate level of protection.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You may access, review or request correction of the Personal Information you have provided by writing to us at help@hailo.co.in. You may withdraw any consent previously given by contacting us in writing; please note that withdrawing consent for certain data may mean we are unable to continue providing the corresponding Service. You may also request a copy of the Personal Information we hold about you, and we will provide it within a reasonable time, subject to verification of your identity.

11. Prohibited Content and Activities

In line with Rule 3(1)(b) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, you agree not to upload, post, transmit or share through the hailO Apps any content that:

  • you do not have the lawful right to share;
  • is obscene, pornographic, paedophilic, defamatory, harassing, or invasive of another person's privacy, including bodily privacy;
  • is harmful to a child;
  • infringes any patent, trademark, copyright or other proprietary right;
  • is knowingly false or misleading and likely to be taken as fact;
  • impersonates any person or misrepresents your affiliation with any person or entity;
  • threatens public order, national security, or the sovereignty and integrity of India, or incites the commission of any offence;
  • contains malicious code, viruses or anything designed to disrupt any computer resource; or
  • otherwise violates any applicable law.
⚖️ Where we determine, acting reasonably, that a user has violated this section or any other provision of this Policy or our Terms and Conditions, we reserve the right to remove the offending content, suspend, or terminate that user's access to the hailO Apps.

12. Children's Privacy

The hailO Apps are intended for use by individuals who are at least 18 years of age or who otherwise have the legal capacity to enter into a binding contract under Indian law. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a minor without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete it.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website or service before providing any information to it.
