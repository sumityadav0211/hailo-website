6.1 The Driver Partner shall promptly proceed to the pickup location after accepting a trip. Repeated cancellations without a valid reason may result in suspension or termination of the Driver Partner's account.

6.3 Where a customer cancels a booking after the Driver Partner has already commenced moving towards the pickup location, hailO shall not be responsible or liable to reimburse the Driver Partner for any fuel, time, or other cost or loss incurred by the Driver Partner on account of such cancellation. hailO may or may not levy a cancellation charge on the customer, at its discretion, and whether or not such a charge is levied, and irrespective of whether any part of it is payable to the Driver Partner, shall be determined by hailO from time to time.

6.4 Where a trip is cancelled by the Driver Partner without a valid reason after having accepted it, the Driver Partner shall be liable to pay such cancellation penalty as may be determined by hailO from time to time.

16.1 All terms of this Agreement apply equally to Platform Services rendered under hailO's Pink Rides (female-only) category, in addition to the specific conditions set out in this Clause.
